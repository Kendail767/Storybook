Morning breaks with roosters crowing and the smell of fry jacks and beans. The village seems normal in daylight – children play with tops, women hang laundry, men head to the milpa.

But you notice:

  • Old Mr. Garcia's prize pig is missing
  • The church door has fresh scratch marks
  • No one will walk past the ceiba tree at the village edge

A small boy runs up. "You the one from the city? Don Chico wants to see you. He's by the shop."
