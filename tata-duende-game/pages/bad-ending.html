You couldn't find the waterfall. Or maybe you couldn't make the sacrifice. Days pass. The village grows quieter. People leave. The forest takes back the milpa, the houses, the church.

Grandma Elodia is the last to go. She finds you sitting on the porch, staring at the jungle.

"It's not your fault," she says gently. "Some curses are too big for one person."

But you know the truth. You could have tried harder. Could have been braver. Could have...

The ceiba tree at the village edge falls in a storm that night. No one plants a new one. The village becomes ghost stories told in other towns. "Did you hear about San Benito Po?" travelers ask. "Tata Duente took it back."

You wander the forest now. Sometimes you see small footprints with backwards feet. Sometimes you hear laughter. You never find your way out.

THE END - The Village is Lost
