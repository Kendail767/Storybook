Don Chico sits on his porch, smoking a cigar despite his 90 years. His eyes are sharp, watching everything.

"So. You come about the trouble." It's not a question.

He gestures to a seat. "Sit. Listen. I tell you what my grandmother told me, and her grandmother told her."

Don Chico speaks for an hour. He tells you about Tata Duente, La Llorona, El Cadejo, Anansi, and the Sisimito. You learn their ways – how to show respect, what to avoid, what to offer.

He leans back. "The curse on this village – someone cut the ceiba at the full moon. Worst possible disrespect. To lift it, you must find Tata Duente's hidden waterfall and make an offering. But the path is dangerous, and the spirits will test you."

He hands you a small pouch. "Copal. Burn it at sacred places. Shows respect."
