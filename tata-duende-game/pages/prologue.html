You step off the bus onto a dusty road. The afternoon sun beats down on the small village of San Benito Po. Grandma Elodia waves from her porch, her smile warm despite the worry in her eyes.

"Come, come, m'ijita," she calls. "So good to see you. But you come at a strange time. The forest is... restless."

You hug her tight, smelling copal and coffee on her dress. In the distance, howler monkeys roar like thunder.

"Restless how?" you ask.

She looks toward the jungle. "Tata Duende. Someone has angered him. The crops won't grow. Animals disappear. Last night, Maria reported seeing La Llorona by the creek again, and she hasn't been seen in years."

You shiver despite the heat.

"Tomorrow, you must visit old Don Chico. He knows the old stories. Maybe he can help us understand. Tonight, rest."
