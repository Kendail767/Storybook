You return to the village without completing the traditional offering, but with a new mission. You gather the children, the parents, the elders.

"Sit down. I'm going to tell you stories."

For weeks, you teach. The story of Tata Duente. La Llorona's tragedy. El Cadejo's warning. Anansi's tricks. The Sisimito's territory.

You take children to the forest, showing them which trees are sacred. Which streams to avoid at night. How to leave offerings.

Slowly, the village remembers.

One morning, you wake to find the crops green and healthy. The air clean. The forest peaceful. At the base of the new ceiba tree, you find a small gift - a jade figurine of a man with a big hat. And a note in crude charcoal:

"Good deal. We're even. Visit sometime. - T.D."

You laugh, and laugh, and laugh.

THE END - The Old Ways Live On
