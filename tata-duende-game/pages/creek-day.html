The creek runs clear and cool over limestone rocks. Small fish dart in the shallows. It's peaceful here. You kneel to drink.

Then you see it - footprints in the mud. But wrong. The heel points forward, but the toes point backward. Backwards feet.

Tata Duente was here. Recently.

The footprints lead upstream, toward a deeper part of the creek.
