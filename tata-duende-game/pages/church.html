The small wooden church smells of incense and old wood. Sunlight streams through simple windows. Father Miguel sweeps the floor.

"Ah, our visitor from the city!" He smiles. "Your grandmother told me you might come. You want to know about the trouble?"

He sets down his broom.

"The Church teaches that these old spirits are just superstition. But..." he lowers his voice, "I've been here forty years. I've seen things. The old ways and the new ways, sometimes they mix."

He dips his finger in a small font. "Take this holy water. It won't hurt any soul from God. As for the rest..." He shrugs. "Can't hurt."
