You return to the village at dawn. As the sun rises, something changes. The air feels lighter. Birds sing louder. In the distance, howler monkeys roar - but it sounds like celebration.

Grandma Elodia meets you at the edge of the village, tears in her eyes. "The crops. They're growing again. Overnight, they GREW."

You show her the ceiba seedling that Tata Duente gave you. Together, you plant it where the old one fell. The whole village watches. Old Don Chico nods, approving.

That night, there's a feast. Fry jacks and stew chicken, rice and beans, potato salad. Music plays - punta, brukdown, everything. Even Father Miguel comes, laughing.

You see small footprints near the new ceiba tree at midnight. Backwards footprints. And beside them, an offering of honey and corn - from Tata Duente, thanking YOU.

You smile, and dance until dawn.

THE END - The Curse is Lifted
