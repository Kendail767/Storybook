After the curse lifts, you can't forget the little forest man. You return to the waterfall often, leaving small gifts. Honey. Sweet corn. Sometimes just stories.

One day, Tata Duente is waiting.

"You keep coming back," he says. "Why?"

You shrug. "I like it here. And... I like you. You're not evil. You're just... lonely."

He's quiet a long time. "My grandmother used to say that. Before everyone forgot us." He looks at you. "Maybe I don't have to be alone. Maybe you could... visit. Sometimes."

You nod. "I will."

And you do. For years, you're the bridge between the village and the forest. When someone gets lost, you know who to ask. When crops fail, you know who to negotiate with. Tata Duente becomes the village's secret guardian, and you become his friend.

The old ways live on, stronger than ever.
