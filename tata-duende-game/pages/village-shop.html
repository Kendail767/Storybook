Donna Marta's shop is tiny but packed – rice, beans, sugar, and surprisingly, a shelf of candles and crosses.

"Eh-eh, look who here!" Donna Marta grins. "Your grandma tell me you coming. You need something?"

You explain about Tata Duende.

Her face grows serious. "Is true. Something bad in the bush. My husband went to check his milpa yesterday and come back white like paper. Said he saw a small man with backwards feet dancing in the corn. When the man saw him, he laughed and disappeared."

She leans close. "You want advice? Get protection. The church has holy water. And take this – my grandmother's cross. It seen off many duendes."

Donna Marta gives you a small wooden cross.
